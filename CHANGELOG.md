## [0.2.0] - 2026-08-25

### Added

- Added a command-line integer expression evaluator with precedence,
  parentheses, checked signed 64-bit arithmetic, and clear errors.
- Added unit coverage for successful expressions and all documented failure
  classes.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-08-25

### Added

## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-25
